About Modo42

What is Modo42?

Modo42 is a training app for people preparing for a HYROX race. It helps you practise the race format, record every split and review how your preparation is going, on your phone and on your watch.

Who it’s for
HYROX athletes, from first-timers to people chasing a championship qualification.
What it does
Race-style and custom simulations, split and station history, performance and readiness tracking, and an athlete profile built around your race.
Platforms
Being built for iPhone and Android, with companion apps for Apple Watch and Wear OS.
Status
In development and not yet in app stores. Join the waitlist for launch news.

What’s inside

Everything between you and the start line.

  • Race-style simulations

    Run a full race simulation or one of three MODO formats, with a live timer for every run, station and transition.

  • Custom simulation builder

    Stack up to 64 runs and stations, set the distance or reps and the weight for each, and save the session for any day.

  • Splits and station progress

    Every saved simulation keeps its splits, so you can see station by station where the seconds go.

  • Performance and readiness

    Practice best, improvement, sessions, readiness and streak, plus a chart of your times against your goal.

  • Athlete profile

    Your race goal, target race, availability, equipment and running background, all in one place.

  • Watch and health data

    Time simulations on Apple Watch or Wear OS, and choose what to share from Apple Health or Health Connect.

FAQ

Questions about Modo42

What is Modo42?

Modo42 is a mobile training companion for HYROX athletes. It brings race-style simulations, a custom simulation builder, split-by-split results and performance tracking together in one app.

Who is Modo42 for?

Athletes preparing for a HYROX race, whether you want to finish your first event, beat your finish time, qualify for championships or simply get HYROX fit.

Can I create my own HYROX simulation?

Yes. The custom simulation builder lets you add up to 64 runs and stations, set the distance or reps and the weight for each, reorder them and save the session to run later.

What performance data does Modo42 show?

Modo42 keeps the splits and station times from every saved simulation. The Performance view shows your practice best, improvement, session count, readiness, sessions this week and streak, with a chart that compares your times to your goal.

Does Modo42 work with Apple Watch or Wear OS?

Yes. Companion apps for Apple Watch and Wear OS let you run a simulation from your wrist with pause, next and end controls, then send the finished session back to your phone.

Is Modo42 available to download?

Not yet. Modo42 is in development for iPhone and Android. Join the waitlist to hear when it’s ready.

How do I join the waitlist?

Enter your email address on the Join the waitlist page.

What is Verified by coach?

Verified by coach is a planned Modo42 feature that will let you send your plan or simulation to a Modo42 coach for review. It isn’t available yet, and we’re recruiting our first coaches now.

Is Modo42 an official HYROX app?

No. Modo42 is an independent training app for HYROX athletes and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by HYROX.
