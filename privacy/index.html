Privacy

Privacy policy.

This policy covers the Modo42 website: the waitlist and the coach interest form. The Modo42 app will have its own privacy policy.

Last updated: Date

Who we are

Modo42 is responsible for the details you give us on this site. Legal entity name and address

Contact us about your data at Privacy contact email.

What we collect

  • Waitlist: your email address.
  • Coach interest form: your name, email address, primary coaching focus, anything you choose to tell us about your experience and qualifications, and the time you agreed to be contacted.
  • With either form, the page you signed up from and the time you signed up.

The site doesn’t use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • Waitlist: to email you when Modo42 is ready to download and with occasional updates about it.
  • Coach interest: to contact you about coaching with Modo42 and to understand who is interested.

We rely on your consent, which you give by submitting the form. We don’t sell your details or use them for anything else.

Where it’s stored

Submissions are stored in a database run by Cloudflare, which hosts this site. When someone registers interest as a coach, their details are also emailed to the Modo42 team through Email provider, e.g. Resend.

How long we keep it

Retention period We delete your details sooner if you ask us to.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the details we hold about you, correct them, or delete them, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Email Privacy contact email and we’ll act on it within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, you can complain to your data protection authority. e.g. the ICO in the UK

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top.
