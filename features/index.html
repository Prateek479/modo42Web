Features

One app from first session to finish line.

Feature overview

  • Race-style simulations

    Run a full race simulation or one of three MODO formats, with a live timer for every run, station and transition.

  • Custom simulation builder

    Stack up to 64 runs and stations, set the distance or reps and the weight for each, and save the session for any day.

  • Splits and station progress

    Every saved simulation keeps its splits, so you can see station by station where the seconds go.

  • Performance and readiness

    Practice best, improvement, sessions, readiness and streak, plus a chart of your times against your goal.

  • Athlete profile

    Your race goal, target race, availability, equipment and running background, all in one place.

  • Watch and health data

    Time simulations on Apple Watch or Wear OS, and choose what to share from Apple Health or Health Connect.

Training

Your week and your race, on one screen.

See your week at a glance, the sessions you’ve completed and a countdown to the HYROX race you’re targeting. Start a simulation in one tap.

  • Week view with your completed sessions
  • Countdown to your target race
  • Workout reminders so sessions don’t slip

Simulation

Rehearse the race, not just the parts.

Run a full race simulation or pick one of three MODO formats. A live timer tracks every run, station and transition, and the result is saved with all of its splits.

What are the MODO formats?

MODO Beginner
About 45 minutes: a 1 km run before each of the first four stations.
MODO Intermediate
About an hour: all eight stations, with a 500 m run before each.
MODO Advanced
No running: all eight stations back to back.

Create

Build a simulation for your weakness.

Can you create your own HYROX simulation? Yes. Open the builder, add the runs and stations you want to work on, and save the session to run on any day.

  • Up to 64 segments in one simulation
  • Distance or reps, plus weight, for each segment
  • Reorder segments until the session is right

Sharing simulations with other athletes is coming soon.

Performance

Where the seconds go.

After every session, Modo42 shows how your preparation is moving and which stations are costing you time.

What performance data does Modo42 show?

Practice best
Your fastest qualifying simulation time.
Improvement
How much time you’ve taken off.
Consistency
Total sessions, sessions this week and your current streak.
Readiness
A readiness score for race day.
Goal chart
Your actual times plotted against your goal time.
Splits
Station-by-station times for every saved simulation, with tips for the stations where you’re furthest behind.

Profile

Made for your goal.

Tell Modo42 about your race and how you train. Your profile keeps the context behind your training and results in one place.

  • Your goal: finish your first HYROX, beat your finish time, qualify for championships or get HYROX fit
  • Target race, race format and division
  • Training availability, equipment, running background and injuries

Watch & health

Your simulation, on your wrist.

Companion apps for Apple Watch and Wear OS show the plans synced from your phone, run the simulation timer with pause, next and end controls, and send the finished session back to your phone.

Health data, on your terms

Connect Apple Health or Health Connect and choose which categories to share, such as workouts, heart and recovery, sleep and body measurements. You can turn sharing off and delete synced data at any time.
